📄 Exam Paper Page 2 (Questions 4, 5)20 Points
Soru 4 Correct the Mistakes in the Text 10 Puan
📌 MEBİ Resmi Öğretmen Çözümü & Model Yanıt:
1. Amy is younger than Emma. → Amy is older than Emma. (16 > 12)
2. Emma is taller than Amy. → Emma is shorter than Amy. (160cm < 165cm)
3. Amy has wavy blond hair and green eyes. → Amy has straight blonde hair and brown eyes.
4. Amy is more outgoing than Emma. → Emma is more outgoing than Amy.
5. Emma doesn't change her mind easily. → Amy doesn't change her mind easily. (She is stubborn.)

Soru 6 Rose's Daily Routine Reading Questions 10 Puan
📌 MEBİ Resmi Öğretmen Çözümü & Model Yanıt:
1. What does Rose do after school?
→ She plays volleyball with her friends.

2. How often does she go hiking?
→ She goes hiking twice a week (on Tuesdays and Thursdays).

3. What does Rose do on Sundays?
→ She usually rides her bike.

4. Does she go to bed early?
→ Yes, she does.

📌 MEBİ Resmi Öğretmen Çözümü & Model Yanıt:

2. I never eat junk food. (0%)
3. I often play basketball in the school garden. (70%)
4. I usually go to the gym on weekdays. (90%)
5. I sometimes go swimming with my friends at the weekend. (50%)

Soru 8 Scott's Routine Reading Questions 10 Puan
📌 MEBİ Resmi Öğretmen Çözümü & Model Yanıt:
1. What time does Scott get up?
→ He always wakes up at seven (7:00) in the morning.

2. What does he do in the evenings?
→ He spends time with his family and they watch movies together.

3. Why does Scott train hard?
→ Because he wants to be a successful football player.
